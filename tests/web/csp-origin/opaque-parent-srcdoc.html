sandboxed srcdoc frame

The frame below has sandbox="allow-scripts", so its document gets a fresh opaque origin; its own srcdoc document inherits/creates that opaque origin. The frame nested inside it has a CSP of its own. allow-scripts is what lets the nested child run: a bare sandbox="" forbids scripting in the whole subtree, and opaque-ness comes from the missing allow-same-origin, not from the flag list being empty.
